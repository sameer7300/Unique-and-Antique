🔧 Common Questions & Solutions

❓ How do I make a product visible to customers?

Go to the product page and make sure the "Status" is set to "Active". Also ensure you have uploaded at least one image and set a price.

❓ Why can't customers see my new product?

Check that: 1) Product status is "Active", 2) You've added product images, 3) Price is set, 4) Stock quantity is greater than 0 if inventory tracking is enabled.

❓ How do I process an order?

Go to Orders section, click on the order, update the status from "Pending" to "Processing" when you start preparing it, then to "Shipped" when sent, and finally "Completed" when delivered.

❓ How do I export my data?

In any list view (Products, Orders, etc.), look for the "Export" button at the top. You can download data as Excel or CSV files for your records.
