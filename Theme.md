Theme Guide for Unique & Antique
1. Color Palette

You want something that conveys heritage + modern luxury (unique + antique).

Primary (Luxury + Trust):

Deep Emerald Green (#0F3D3E) OR Royal Blue (#1C3879)

Secondary (Warm Contrast):

Antique Gold (#D4AF37) OR Copper Bronze (#B87333)

Neutral Base:

Soft Off-White (#F5F5F0)

Charcoal Gray (#2C2C2C)

👉 Why?

Green/Blue = trust + sophistication.

Gold/Copper = antique/luxury feel.

Neutral = keeps products in focus.

2. Typography

Headings (Premium Serif): Playfair Display or Merriweather.

Antique/classic vibe for product titles, hero sections.

Body (Clean Sans): Inter or Poppins.

Modern readability for descriptions, checkout, reviews.

Accent (Monogram): Custom U&A logo font for favicon or section dividers.

3. Layout

Homepage

Hero Banner (animated text fade-in + product highlight carousel).

Featured Categories (grid with antique hover effect).

Trending Products (smart recommendation carousel).

Testimonials / Reviews (scrollable with soft animations).

Newsletter Signup (minimalistic with antique border design).

Product Page

Clean 2-column layout (large product photo left, details right).

Interactive zoom & 360° view for certain products.

Rating + Review stars in gold.

Add-to-cart animation (smooth fly-to-cart effect).

Cart & Checkout

Progress bar (“Cart → Address → Payment → Done”).

Easypaisa / Stripe / COD options with brand icons.

Order summary card with elegant borders.

4. Animations & Effects

Microinteractions:

Buttons have a gold underline slide on hover.

Cart icon shakes slightly when a product is added.

Stars in reviews animate with a soft “glow” when hovered.

Page transitions:

Smooth fade/slide (Framer Motion with Next.js).

Hero Banner Animation:

Antique border pattern slowly fades in the background.

5. Smart Features (Industrial-Grade Touch)

Personalized Recommendations (Django backend ML).

Recently Viewed Products section with subtle fade-in.

Dynamic Filters & Search: AJAX-powered, instant filtering.

Dark Mode Toggle: Antique gold pops beautifully on dark backgrounds.

6. Favicon & Logo Integration

Logo (luxury serif + monogram).

Favicon = simplified U&A monogram in gold, transparent background.

Used as a watermark in subtle backgrounds
